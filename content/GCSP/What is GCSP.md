The Grand Challenges Scholars Program (GCSP) is an organization that's designed to create well-rounded engineers who are ready to tackle the worlds greatest engineering challenges, as specified by the National Academy of Engineers (NAE). Graduating as a GSCP scholar requires completing the five pillars of the GCSP. By completing these pillars, GCSP claims that graduates have a strong background that will make them an excellent engineer.
